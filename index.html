FFS 2027 Catalog, Work in Progress

This is an early draft of the 2027 catalog, shared for internal review. It builds on the visual style we approved earlier and extends it into a full catalog. Please treat this as one piece of a broader ecosystem: catalog, marketing sheets, technical data sheets, flyers and more.

The concept: from sketch to reality

The section and product covers draw on the design process of Chip Foose, who sketches a vehicle by hand before bringing it to life. Fast Fill Systems works the same way: we take an idea and engineer it into a real, field-ready product. That sketch-to-reality spirit is what the covers are meant to convey. The concept is designed to carry the brand for a while and can evolve over time. Nothing is set in stone.

New: complete systems

Beyond individual products, this catalog now presents the complete systems Fast Fill Systems offers, such as pressureless configurations. These pages show how the components work together inside the tank, making it easier to understand the full solution rather than just a single part.

What to review at this stage

Please focus on layout, visual style and overall structure. Technical content has not yet been validated by Engineering, so specifications, dimensions and product names may contain inconsistencies. Some images are AI-generated and may include technical inaccuracies.

Still in progress

  • Section and product covers are provisional. For example, the Fuel Nozzles cover may eventually show the full nozzle lineup.
  • Some interior pages for each product line are still being designed.
  • QR codes currently link to the nozzles only (Atlas, PitBoss, SureLoc 150 and SureLoc 1000). Other products will be added in the same way. In the digital version, QR codes will also work as clickable links.
